i

Where we stand, and why timing is the whole game

spawnd.dev today: Authority Score 2, zero organic traffic, 16 ranked keywords, 37 backlinks from 32 referring domains. The marketing site has four indexable pages (landing, /security, /download, /legion), no sitemap, no robots file, no blog, and the docs live only in the GitHub repo. As an SEO asset the domain does not exist yet.

The nearest rival, Happy (happy.engineering), sits at Authority Score 27 with ~3,500 organic visits/mo across 433 keywords and 556 referring domains — respectable, and entirely catchable. That is roughly the profile spawnd.dev should expect after a successful Show HN: a front-page HN launch typically lands 200–600 referring domains within weeks, which is exactly the jump from AS 2 to AS ~25–30.

The one structural insight

The Halloween launch is not just a distribution event — it is the domain's link event. Link equity flows to whatever pages exist when the links arrive, and it compounds from that day. Every page in this document that exists on October 31 starts ranking in November. Every page built in November waits months longer for the same position.

Ship the SEO surface before the launch, not after it. Ten weeks is enough for all of Tier 0–2.

ii

Method

Semrush Keyword Magic Tool (logged-in session, US database, broad match, all-keywords mode), 37 seed queries spanning the product's demand space: the agent-CLI ecosystem (claude code, codex cli, opencode, aider, claude max), remote terminal access (ssh from browser, web terminal, remote terminal, per-device SSH), the phone-coding surface (mobile ide, coding on ipad), the DIY stack SPAWN D productizes (tmux, mosh, tailscale, vscode remote ssh), competitors (termius, happy coder, omnara), and category terms (ai coding agent, background agents, self hosted ai, agent orchestration).

For each seed: the top 100 rows by volume with volume, KD, CPC, intent, and competitive density — 3,086 unique keywords carrying search volume after dedupe. Cluster sizing sums only keywords at KD≤50 with ≥50 searches/mo, so every aggregate below is a floor, not a projection: the full seed universes run to hundreds of thousands of long-tail variants the top-100 sample never touches (the remote access universe alone is 455K keywords).

viii

Foundation — make the domain crawlable before making it famous

  1. Sitemap + robots. Neither exists. app/sitemap.ts and app/robots.ts in the Next.js app; an afternoon.
  2. Docs on-site, not only in the repo. TRUST.md, SESSIOND.md, and the exorcism doc rendered under spawnd.dev/docs. “Designing a control plane that can't read your data” is a linkable essay the GTM plan already intends to post — every link it earns should land on spawnd.dev, not github.com.
  3. A blog that matches the register. The grimoire voice is a durable differentiator in a sea of AI-written SEO sludge — deadpan, mechanism-first, quotable. Every page above ends with the same honest turn: here's the DIY way in full, and here's the one-line possession.
  4. Structured data + llms.txt. FAQ/HowTo schema on guides; an llms.txt naming what spawnd is in one paragraph. AI-search visibility is currently zero across every engine Semrush tracks, and definitional pages plus calculators are precisely what assistants cite.
  5. Internal linking discipline. Hubs link spokes, spokes link the product page for their use case, every tool page links its explainer. At AS 2, internal links are most of the link graph — spend them deliberately.
